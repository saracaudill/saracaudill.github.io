I want to evoke ancient Greek [black-figure pottery](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/248902) in the art for *Firetaker*, so I've been using mask layers in Photoshop to create solid-color outlines.

Here's an example of what I mean:

![Helmaroc King](/assets/img/blog-07-img-01.png)

When I picture what my Stymphalian Bird looks like, I see the Helmaroc King from *The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker*. This particular reference image is by [Fil Romero](https://www.deviantart.com/forte-girl7/art/The-Helmaroc-King-317061702).

![Stymphalian Bird pencil sketch](/assets/img/blog-07-img-02.png)

From there, I do a rough pencil sketch on whatever paper I have handy.

![Final illustration](/assets/img/blog-07-img-03.png)

Finally, I import the sketch into Photoshop, use the paint bucket to add a solid-fill layer, and "erase" the line art with masking.

I plan to do all of the art for the three playtest scenarios, as well as for the core rulebook. Wish me luck.
