I think of logo designers the same way I think of musicians: as wizards whose powers are beyond my mortal ken.

However, I also struggle to give up total ownership of the design process. As such, it took me entirely too long to admit that I had neither the background nor the patience to produce the *Firetaker* logo.

I used the *Fatherfog* logo as my initial inspiration because it’s clever and scalable. It tells you exactly what kind of game it's going to be from appearance alone.

![fatherfog 0e logo](/assets/img/blog-03-img-01.png)

And even though I haven’t watched it since I was a kid, I still often think about the distinctive, high-contrast art style of Samurai Jack. I aimed to create a logo that simultaneously resembled Aku’s hand reaching for a ball of fire while also reading clearly as an ‘F.'

![aku from samurai jack](/assets/img/blog-03-img-02.png)

![logo exploration](/assets/img/blog-03-img-03.png)

It was a bit of an overengineered mess. However, I realized the clawed hand itself could look like flames. I dropped the ‘F’ prerequisite and decided to focus on a logomark instead of a logotype. [Recent Assassin’s Creed titles](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Assassin%27s_Creed_Odyssey_Logo.png) lock their respective logos in place by enclosing them within the series’ unmistakable emblem. Zeus seeks to snuff out the lives of the foolish mortals who defied him, so I thought using an Omega symbol would be appropriate.

And then, after all that, I realized I wanted to focus on the writing, art, and layout and not a damn logo. So that’s when I reached out to my fellow game designers on the Lionhearth Exploration Outfitters Discord server and enlisted the help of someone who actually likes making logos. Thanks, [Akumata](https://akumata.itch.io/), for helping me establish *Firetaker’s* visual identity. I would not/could not have done it without you.
