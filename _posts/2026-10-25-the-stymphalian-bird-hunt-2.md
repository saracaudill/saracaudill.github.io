Success! I finished the first playtest adventure for *Firetaker*! I've posted it to itch as a [free download](https://saracaudill.itch.io/the-stymphalian-bird-hunt).

![Page 1 of The Stymphalian Bird Hunt](/assets/img/blog-09-img-01.jpg)

![Page 2 of The Stymphalian Bird Hunt](/assets/img/blog-09-img-02.jpg)

I haven't played in a hexcrawl in almost five years, and I've never run one. I'm hopeful that the encounter and terrain tables provide the right balance of tension and exploration. However, even if it winds up being a slog, that's valuable info, too. It's a playtest scenario for playtest rules. There are going to be rough spots. Better to catch the blemishes now when the only thing on the line is my own time.
