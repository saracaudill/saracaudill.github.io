I didn't make progress on *Firetaker* this week, unless you consider reading some Aeschylus progress. *Prometheus Bound* did give the eponymous titan more human qualities (e.g., stubbornness, pettiness) than I'm used to, so I suppose that provided some food for thought regarding the game's inciting incident.

But mostly, I've been distracted. A previous me would hold it against myself. Current me recognizes that distractions (the fun kind) underpin my creativity and design thinking. I've been distracted by a *Pathfinder 1e* Adventure Path I might play with some friends, as well as by the release of the *Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* remake. Both are intense passions (my wife would call them all-consuming) that influence the kinds of games I want to make and the kinds of stories I want to tell.

So am I justifying my lack of forward momentum on *Firetaker*? Yes. Do I feel guilty about it? Okay, maybe a little, but mostly nah.
