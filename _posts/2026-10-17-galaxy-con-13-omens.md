I didn't post anything last week because I was at [Galaxy Con Nightmare Weekend](https://galaxycon.com/pages/nightmare-weekend-raleigh) with my pals at [Find the Path Ventures](https://find-path.com/). The con was about all things horror and featured guests like Elvira and the *Terrifier* clown.

![Mothman](/assets/img/blog-08-img-01.png)

While I'm not a horror fan, I *am* a horror TTRPG fan. Someone over the weekend asked how I could love games like *Mothership* and *Call of Cthulhu* if I can't sit through *The Nightmare Before Christmas* or play *The Last of Us*. For me, it's a matter of agency. I get to imagine how ghoulish the murder scene is. I get to devise my own solution for thwarting the eldritch abomination. I get to choose to run out of the house like a rational person rather than up the stairs.

So while I wasn't interested in getting horror celebrity autographs, I *was* hyped to play *13 Omens*, Jason Bulmahn's new horror TTRPG. Rick ran *The Haunting of Hollybrook* for us, and I later played a 45-minute demo with Rue Dickey, Paizo's Head of Marketing. *13 Omens* is fast, light, and economical. The shared dice pool, three-act structure, and archetypal Player Characters make it ideal for pick-up-and-play. I'd recommend it as a backup game for folks who can't get their whole group together for their regular game. I'd also recommend it for tricking non-gamer friends and family into playing a TTRPG.
