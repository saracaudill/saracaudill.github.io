![Original and final versions of cover art](/assets/img/blog-10-img-01.png)

The tone is all off in my original cover. I was going for foreboding, but I think the watercolors made it seem too soft. I wanted dread, not whimsy.

The island illustration also didn't sell the hook well, either. Yes, the scenario takes place on an island, but a GM is going to pick up this pamphlet if they want to harass their players with a big, man-eating bird that shoots metal feathers. Actually, *that* was enticing imagery. I swapped the soft tones for stark black-and-white, the island vibes for a feather dripping blood. It fits.
